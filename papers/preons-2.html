abstract

The follow-up to the dual-polarity preon toy model asks the harder question: in which regions of parameter space can the framework live at all? The paper works through parameter-space constraints on the composite neutral pairs and identifies the regimes in which an emergent acoustic metric remains viable, sharpening the original proposal into something that can be confronted with observational and collider bounds.
