03 · Reichweitenmessung

GoatCounter, cookielos

Zur Reichweitenmessung nutze ich GoatCounter. Der Dienst setzt keine Cookies, verwendet kein Fingerprinting und legt keine Profile über Besucherinnen und Besucher an. Erfasst werden ausschließlich aggregierte Angaben wie aufgerufene Seite, Referrer, ungefähre Bildschirmgröße, Browser und Land.

Ihre IP-Adresse wird von GoatCounter nicht gespeichert. Zur Unterscheidung von Sitzungen wird ein Hash gebildet, der täglich mit einem wechselnden Zufallswert neu erzeugt und danach verworfen wird. Eine Wiedererkennung über einen Tag hinaus oder über mehrere Websites hinweg ist damit nicht möglich.

Weil keine Informationen auf Ihrem Endgerät gespeichert oder ausgelesen werden, ist hierfür keine Einwilligung nach § 25 TDDDG erforderlich. Rechtsgrundlage für die Verarbeitung der aggregierten Daten ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO; das berechtigte Interesse liegt darin, zu verstehen, welche Inhalte gelesen werden. Weitere Angaben in der Datenschutzübersicht von GoatCounter.

Privacy notice

This is a personal professional and research site. It sets no cookies, embeds no advertising networks, and loads no fonts or scripts from Google. The German text above is the authoritative version; this English summary is provided for convenience.

Controller

Michél Nguyen, Thuringia, Germany. For privacy enquiries, please use the contact form. Full provider details under § 5 DDG will be added once the legal review is complete.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA). Loading a page necessarily transmits your IP address, timestamp, requested file, referrer, browser and operating system to GitHub's servers. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — the legitimate interest in operating the site securely and reliably. I have no control over the retention of GitHub's server logs. Transfers to the USA rest on standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Analytics

Visitor counting uses GoatCounter, which sets no cookies, does no fingerprinting, and builds no visitor profiles. It records aggregate information only: page, referrer, approximate screen size, browser and country. Your IP address is not stored; sessions are distinguished by a hash generated with a salt that rotates daily and is then discarded, so no recognition is possible beyond a single day or across sites. Because nothing is stored on or read from your device, no consent under § 25 TDDDG is required. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. See GoatCounter's privacy overview.

Contact form

Name, email address, subject and message are processed for delivery through Formspree, Inc. (Wilmington, DE, USA) as a processor, with transfers covered by standard contractual clauses. The data is used solely to answer your enquiry and any follow-up, never for marketing. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message concerns a prospective contract or role, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f). Enquiries are deleted once handled, absent statutory retention duties. A hidden anti-spam field is filled only by automated scripts. See Formspree's privacy policy.

Local storage and fonts

No cookies are used. Switching the view (executive or operator) or the language stores that choice in your browser's local storage, as minh.systems:view and minh.systems:lang, so the site looks the way you left it. These values never leave your device and are not analysed; you can clear them in your browser settings at any time. The webfonts (JetBrains Mono, Inter) are served from this origin — there is no connection to Google Fonts or any other font network, and your IP address is not passed to a third party for them.

External links

The site links out to DOI records, GitHub, ORCID, Google Scholar and LinkedIn, among others. Those providers are responsible for any processing on their own pages; nothing is transmitted to them before you follow a link.

Your rights

Subject to the statutory conditions you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). No automated decision-making or profiling takes place. You may also complain to a supervisory authority — the Thuringian Data Protection Commissioner is competent here, and you may equally approach the authority where you live.
