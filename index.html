Why SkeinDB ships a single binary

I keep collapsing sidecars back into the database binary because evaluation speed matters more than architectural ceremony at this stage. If the admin plane, runtime evidence, and protocol adapters live in different processes, every claim about the system arrives fragmented. A single executable forces the product, the research surface, and the operational story to line up in one place, which makes the project easier to inspect and much harder to hand-wave.
